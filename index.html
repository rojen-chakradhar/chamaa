faq

What is the minimum order?

The minimum order is 10 pieces per brownie flavor. You can order more using the quantity selector or by typing your desired quantity.

How do i place an order?

Choose your brownies, add then to your cart, fill out the checkout form, and click send order from WhatsApp. We'll receive your order details there and confirm everything with you.

Can I order different brownie flavors?

Yes. You can add multiple flavors to your cart and choose different quantities for each one.

Can I request something specifi with my order?

Absolutely. You can mention allergies, special requests, delivery instructions, or anything else in notes section during checkout. We'll discuss/confirm it with you on WhatsApp.

How will my order be confirmed?

Submitting the checkout form doesn't automatically finalize your order. It sends your order details to WhatsApp, where we'll review the details and confirm the order with you.

How do I pay?

You can pay with Esewa, Khalti, Mobile Banking, or cash on delivery.

Do you deliver?

Yes, we offer delivery within our service area. Delivery availability and charges will be confirmed through WhatsApp.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on your location and order schedule. We'll provide an estimated delivery time when confirming your order on WhatsApp.
